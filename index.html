About Me

I am a master's student at The University of Manchester, pursuing an MSc in Robotics with a focus on the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence.

In addition, I have a strong interest in software development and engineering, and enjoy building applications that solve real-world problems. Beyond my academic work, I have a keen interest in game development, where I like to experiment with interactive design and storytelling through technology.

Autonomous Table Tennis Collecting Robot

In this project, we developed an autonomous robot that detects and collects table tennis balls using the YOLOv5 object-detection model. I created a custom dataset, integrated perception and motion control modules, and optimized navigation and obstacle avoidance to ensure stable and efficient operation.

Menu of the Integrated Robot

In this project, our team built a multifunctional mobile robot capable of obstacle avoidance, line following, and remote control. I integrated all functional modules into a unified system and implemented a menu-based GUI for intuitive operation. I also developed and debugged the remote-control program, ensuring smooth interaction and stable performance.

Movie Recommendation System Based on Conversational Agent

In this project, we built a localized movie recommendation system using generative AI. I fine-tuned the model to retrieve data from a specified database and produce clear, user-friendly recommendations in a structured format.

YOLOv10-based Model for Player and Football Detection

In this project, I improved YOLOv10 for small-object detection (players, soccer balls) by redesigning the detector structure and optimizing key layers. The resulting paper was accepted by the International Journal of Computer Science and Information Technology (2024).

Coffee Shop Simulator

In this project, our team developed a multi-threaded coffee shop simulation system with real-time GUI updates to model interactions among customers, servers, and baristas. I implemented order calculation, designed the class structure and data models, developed the online order module, and optimized queue management and system performance.

The Effects of Levelling System on Video Games

In this project, I examined how different level progression systems affect player experience by integrating three designs into one framework, running playtests, and analyzing volunteer feedback to evaluate their impact on engagement and gameplay.

Robotics and the Sustainable Development Goals

A compulsory individual coursework piece intended to force a connection between robotics and the UN Sustainable Development Goals — essentially a box-ticking exercise rather than real engineering work. I produced the entire video from start to finish on my own: research, scripting, recording, editing, and narration.

LiDAR Parameter Testing and Analysis

An individual coursework experiment ostensibly meant to develop some kind of experimental analysis skill, in which we were asked to test and analyse several parameters of a LiDAR sensor. I carried out the experiments, processed the results, and produced the entire video myself.

Video

Leo Rover — Autonomous Block Detection and Pickup

A team project in which we built a rover capable of identifying coloured target blocks, autonomously navigating to them, and picking them up. I was responsible for the block detection system — developing and tuning the HSV-based colour detection pipeline to work across both the Gazebo simulation and the physical RealSense hardware, and integrating goal forwarding into the Nav2 stack.
